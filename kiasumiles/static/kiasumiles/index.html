Request-scoped by design

Your stack stays under your agent's control.

Your agent or local client holds your selected card products under its own controls and supplies them when it asks for a lookup. Hosted KiasuMiles ranks that request and does not store your stack.

  • Your agent decides how and where your selection is remembered.
  • Only the supplied products are considered for that lookup.
  • KiasuMiles returns the recommendation and its practical caveats.
